🚍 How to Get from Bolton to Toronto by Transit

Living in Bolton and need to get to Toronto? Whether it’s for work, shopping, or to go to a Leafs game (Go, Leafs, Go! 🍁), you don’t always need a car to make the trip. Thanks to Route 41 Bolton, Route 501 Züm Queen BRT, and the TTC Line 1 subway, there’s a smooth and affordable way to connect Caledon to downtown Toronto.

🛰️ Step 1: Track Your Bus & Walk To Your Stop with Caledon Transit

Before you even head out the door, the best way to start your trip is by checking Caledon Transit.

Plan ahead so you’re not rushing — buses can arrive earlier or later depending on traffic.


👉 You can do all this directly at

caledontransit.com

💡 Pro Tip: Arrive at your stop at least 5 minutes early and have your PRESTO card ready to tap.


🚌 Step 2: Ride Route 41 Bolton to Highway 50 Zum Queen Station

Once you’ve tracked your bus and found your stop, hop on Route 41 Bolton. This route takes you from Bolton to Highway 50 Züm Queen Station, where you’ll make your next connection. Sit back and relax 🙃
