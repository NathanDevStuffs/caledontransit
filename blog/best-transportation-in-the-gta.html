What’s the best method of transportation in the GTA?

Posted Wednesday, October 01, 2025



Photo by Zia Syed on Unsplash

In Toronto, Canada, we have a lot of different transportation methods such as the classy car, public transportation, expensiiive rideshares, and biking. But what is really the best method?

Well lets dive straight into cars and lets talk about the pros. First off, cars are really convenient, like really convenient. You can just hop in your car and drive to your destination, like point A to point B. No need to walk to a bus stop or walk to a metro station.

Also, you can go where you want, when you want without having to check public transit schedules. Another advantage is comfort. If you don’t want some other person’s shoulders touching your shoulders or you just wanna be in control of the radio and control the fresh fresh air conditioning then a car seems pretty comfy if you ask me.

Now let’s get into the cons. If you’re from Toronto, you’d know the pain of going on the Don Valley and spending an eternity waiting for traffic. Also let's not mention gas prices, sky-high car insurance and the risk of you getting in a car accident which makes the car very very pricey.

Now we’ll move over to the bike and we’ll start off with the pros. A huge major advantage is that bikes can weave through traffic and have bike lanes that essentially skip traffic. That means for short trips, your rusty old bike might actually be faster than a car in some areas, which is pretty sweet! Also, getting a bike is way cheaper than getting a car and you are contributing to reducing traffic which is a win-win!

Bikes also have their share of cons too. First off, right-hook collisions are common and dooring is a pretty big issue, which can injure a cyclist pretty badly. Also, bike lanes in the GTA usually end abruptly or never connect with each other, forcing bikers to share the road with car drivers where cars may speed up right beside them which might scare bikers.

Last but not least, let’s move over to public transit. First off, public transportation, for the most part, is cheaper than owning a car. Also, it moves people wayy more efficiently than private cars. For example, one TTC subway train can replace 910 private cars in rush hour! That also means that by taking public transportation, you’re contributing to reducing traffic congestion.

Though public transportation also has its cons too. Right off the bat, even though a subway train can replace 910 cars, it’s going to be hella jam-packed on that train which can be pretty uncomfortable. Also, public transit sometimes is pretty slow, and is not as flexible as having a bike or car as you may have to walk to your final destination or walk a bit to your bus stop.

Now with all that being said, we can say that the best transportation method in the GTA is… (drumroll please)... well it depends. Bikes are pretty good for short trips, like getting a couple groceries from your local NoFrills or commuting Downtown Toronto as it’s a complete nightmare. Now public transit is pretty good for medium trips, like commuting downtown to Vaughan. Now cars are pretty good for travelling long distances and if you prefer the flexibility! However, we can make public transit better by investing in more rail projects, bus rapid transit projects and light metros. So maybe in the future, we’ll have a region where transit is higher than cars.
